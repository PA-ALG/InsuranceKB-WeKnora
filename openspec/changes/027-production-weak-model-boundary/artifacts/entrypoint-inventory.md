## Packaging and command exposure check

- `harness/pyproject.toml` defines no `[project.scripts]` or model-related entry-point
  group. The relevant commands are currently reachable through `python -m ...`, so
  module callables and package exports—not console-script registration—are the bypass
  surface.
- `harness/src/insurance_harness/compiler/__init__.py` publicly exports
  `OpenAICompatClient`, `ModelClient`, `MeteredClient`, `ReplayClient` and lazy pipeline
  symbols. The generic `LiteLLMClient` is directly importable from `compiler.llm` even
  though it is absent from compiler `__all__`.
- `harness/src/insurance_harness/goldenset/__init__.py` publicly exports
  `GoldenAnnotator`, `annotate_product`, `ModelClient`, `ReplayClient` and
  `LiteLLMClient` (alongside deterministic golden/evaluation helpers). Every model or
  replay surface in this package is explicit offline/goldenset/replay. It does not
  export `run_020`, but `python -m insurance_harness.goldenset.run_020` remains a direct
  offline Golden module entrypoint.
- `harness/src/insurance_harness/knowledge/__init__.py` was checked item-by-item for
  state-advancing exports. The relevant importer, ChangeSet/Claim, review/conflict,
  source-lifecycle and release functions are listed above. They remain zero-model and
  enforce their own governance contracts; they must not acquire direct
  `IssuedModelPermit`, `ModelPolicy`, `AdmissionBinding` or receipt wiring.
- `harness/src/insurance_harness/live_env/__init__.py` exports nothing; the probe is
  still directly importable from `live_env.model_probe` and is not an admission
  authority.

## Freeze decision

The inventory freezes three disjoint classes: (1) production model paths that receive
canonical `GuardedModelClient` from production composition and call it only with
`VerifiedAdmission + ModelCallContext`; its internal policy alone issues opaque
`IssuedModelPermit` and emits receipt-only `ModelPermitView`; (2) explicit
offline/goldenset/replay/manual paths that cannot be production fallback, promotion
authority or production admission; and (3) zero-model product/knowledge paths that
enforce only their own governance, approval and snapshot contracts. Callers never pass
permit, policy, issuer or guard. Zero-model entrypoints receive no `IssuedModelPermit`,
`ModelPolicy`, `AdmissionBinding` or receipt parameters. A probe, transport call,
offline judgement, Golden run or artifact content is never sufficient to derive
production admission.
